CaloSnap UI Design v1.1

The base is Garden: warm ivory, forest green, soft cards. From Ledger it takes horizontal macro bars, the ingredient ledger, the calorie range scale and basis indicators. From Lens it takes the dark camera, framing guidance and fast Precision questions. Food photography is shown as labeled placeholders.

Estimates, never exact
Calories appear as a range with a most-likely value. Each ingredient is marked Seen, Estimated or Assumed.
Nothing counts until saved
Scans are drafts. Daily totals change only after Save on the Results review. Meal fit shows the impact as a projected, hatched segment.
Minimal input
Auto asks nothing. Precision asks up to three one-tap questions, and every one can be skipped or ended early.
User-owned targets
Recommended and manual targets stay separate. Profile changes never overwrite a manual target.

Open decisions before handoff
  1. Photo retention: discard after analysis by default (current design), or keep a small thumbnail for History?
  2. Sign-in for the private MVP: email magic link, Apple/Google, or invite code?
  3. Daily scan limit for cost control: should the limit be visible (A2 variant) or silent?
  4. Upper age bound and other eligibility rules for automatic targets (pregnancy and medical conditions are deferred).
  5. Nutrition Goal Engine spec: energy equation, activity factors, goal adjustments, macro rules and rounding. All values shown are placeholders.
  6. Third-party vision provider choice and its data-retention terms, which the consent copy needs.
  7. Icon set: placeholder shapes are used throughout. Pick a library (e.g. Phosphor or Lucide) at handoff.
